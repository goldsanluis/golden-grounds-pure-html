Our Story

More Than a Coffee Shop

Golden Grounds was born from the belief that great coffee deserves a great stage. We source our beans from single-origin farms, brew with intention, and serve in a space where artists, poets, and neighbours come together.

Every cup we craft, every performance we host — it all carries the same golden touch.
